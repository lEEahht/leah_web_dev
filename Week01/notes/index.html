buttermilk pancakes

Ingredients

  • 3 japanese sweet potatoes
  • 3 table spoons of oliove oil
  • tin foil
  • 2 tea spoon salt

Instructions

  1. preheat oven to 325 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Wash sweet potatoes and pat dry.
  3. Prick each sweet potato several times with a fork.
  4. Rub each sweet potato with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
  5. Wrap each sweet potato in aluminum foil.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until tender, about 1.5 to 2 hours depending on size.
  7. Remove from oven and let cool slightly before unwrapping.
  8. Serve warm and enjoy!
