This is a simple and delicious recipe for Japanese sweet potatoes. They are naturally sweet and buttery, perfect for snacking or as a side dish.

Japanese Sweet Potato

This recipe is fun and simple. Packed with many nutrients you can take your sweet potato as a snack of as a side for you. Japanese sweet potatoes are rich in antioxidants and essential vitamins such as B vitamins, C, and E — potentially providing various health benefits including weight management and mood improvement.

Ingredients

  • 3 Japanese sweet potatoes
  • 1 Tablespoon Olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Wash and scrub the sweet potatoes thoroughly.
  3. Prick each sweet potato several times with a fork.
  4. Rub the sweet potatoes with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
  5. Place the sweet potatoes on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  6. Bake for 45-60 minutes, or until they are tender when pierced with a fork.
