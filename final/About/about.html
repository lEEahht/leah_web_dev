ABOUT   ME

Hi, I'm Leah Tewolde — a passionate engineer at the intersection of technology and creativity. I love building things that are as thoughtful as they are powerful.

I specialize in software engineering and design, blending technical rigor with a strong visual sensibility. Every project I take on reflects my commitment to craftsmanship, clarity, and impact.

What drives me is a belief that engineering should serve people and the planet. I'm passionate about clean energy systems, sustainable technology, and building software that creates real-world impact.

I want to use computer science as a tool for equity — making powerful technology accessible, beautiful, and genuinely useful for communities that need it most.
