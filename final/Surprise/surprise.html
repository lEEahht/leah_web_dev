ANIMATED SHORT FILM

For this project I muted the original audio and recreated sound for the video using foley and background music. Some notable sounds were the clinking sound of the can. I used a pot from my kitchen and time manipulation to speed it up and make it high pitched to match the speed of the rock. My favorite part was the little voice on the character. I took a clip of a child's voice and sped it up to make it sound like the character is talking to himself.
